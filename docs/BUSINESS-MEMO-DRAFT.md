## 3. Business model and payment flow

1. The customer selects one or more fixed-price services on the website and adds them to a cart. No account is required (guest checkout).
2. At checkout the customer gives a name, email and phone number and accepts the Terms, Privacy, Refund & Cancellation and Service Delivery policies.
3. Payment is collected through Airpay on Airpay's hosted payment page. The website never handles card, UPI PIN or banking credentials.
4. The order is marked paid only after server-to-server verification with Airpay and an amount check. Mismatched amounts are held for manual review.
5. The team contacts the customer, confirms the scope in writing, and delivers the service remotely (report, configuration work, files). No physical goods are shipped and there are no subscriptions.
6. Refunds and cancellations are handled manually on email request, under the published Refund & Cancellation Policy.
