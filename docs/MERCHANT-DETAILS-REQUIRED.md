# Merchant Details Required

For: Authentic Media and IT Sector Private Limited (director / company secretary).
Prepared: 14 November 2026, Phase 3. Reference: `authenticmedia.report.pdf` (14 Nov 2026).

The website cannot be submitted for bank re-screening until every **Mandatory** item below
is supplied from the company's own documents. Nothing here has been guessed or filled in.

## How to supply values

Send each value together with the document it was copied from. The developer enters it in
`src/lib/site.ts` → `LEGAL`; nothing else needs editing. Empty values render nothing, so the
site never shows a placeholder.

**Do not send** KYC documents for publication (PAN, Aadhaar, director ID proofs, bank
statements, board resolutions). They are evidence for the bank, not website content. Only
the values listed below are published.

## A. Statutory identifiers — Mandatory

| # | Value | Source document | Format check (automated) | Published on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **CIN** | Certificate of Incorporation / MCA master data | `L/U` + 5 digits + state code + year + 3 letters + 6 digits, e.g. `U12345UP2020PTC123456` | Footer, /about company card, company block on all 5 policies and /grievance-redressal |
| A2 | **GSTIN** | GST Registration Certificate (Form GST REG-06) | 15 characters, e.g. `09ABCDE1234F1Z5`; the first two digits for Uttar Pradesh are `09` | Same as A1 |

If the company is **not GST-registered**, say so in writing. The site then shows no GSTIN,
and the legal reviewer must confirm that this is acceptable and that prices carry no GST
(see LEGAL-APPROVAL-CHECKLIST §D).

## B. Directors — Mandatory

One entry per director listed on the MCA master data.

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Full name | Yes | As registered with MCA |
| Designation | Yes | e.g. Director, Managing Director, Whole-time Director |
| Verified professional background | Yes | One or two factual sentences the director confirms (prior roles, field of experience). No unverifiable claims ("industry leader", years of experience that cannot be evidenced). |
| DIN | Optional | Publish only if the company chooses to |

Published on: /about → "Leadership" (appears automatically once at least one director is entered).

## C. Grievance Officer — Mandatory

Required by the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. The person must be **formally
designated by the company**. Naming them on the website is not a designation.

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | |
| Designation | Yes | Their role in the company (the code requires this field too) |
| Official email | Yes | A company mailbox that someone monitors, ideally `grievance@authenticmedia.fun` or similar. Not a personal Gmail. |
| Phone | Yes | A number answered during business hours |

Published on: /grievance-redressal. Once these are set, that page's complaint email and phone
switch from the general contact details to the officer's.

## E. Optional

- Business hours for phone support.
- Refund and grievance turnaround commitments the company is prepared to meet (for example,
  acknowledge within 48 hours). Only commit to what can be met. See LEGAL-APPROVAL-CHECKLIST §C.
- Data-retention periods (Privacy Policy currently says they are not yet fixed).
- Real social-media profile URLs, if they are to be linked.
- A backend inbox for the contact form (it currently opens the visitor's email app).
